## What this pass did not do

It did not read the 17 incidents-and-funding records that rest on Wikipedia
(README item 4) beyond the ones that surfaced above, and it did not touch the
`ofcom_investigates_x_over_grok_deepfakes_2026` date conflict, which sits
inside the clean 61 and is the strongest candidate for the next pass.

One mechanism note, offered rather than acted on. Two of the corrections here
-- pair 7's phantom one-day conflict and the Southaven/Memphis venue -- were
found by fetching a URL the record already carried and comparing it to the
record's own prose. Nothing mechanical checks that a record's description
agrees with the source it cites, and nothing can in general. But the narrower
claim *"every date asserted in a description also appears in a fetched source"*
is closer to checkable than it looks, and it is the class of error both of
these were.
